# AirStats 1.7.0

## 中文

- 将 Pulse AI 额度监控作为 AirStats 内置功能整合，提供服务商/账号配置、额度窗口、消费历史与告警设置。
- 状态栏 AI 读数新增紧凑圆环和多时段圆环，可在“设置 → 菜单栏 → AI 额度 → 显示方式”切换；默认圆环显示当前额度百分比，多时段样式显示最多两个额度窗口。
- 状态栏继续使用 AirStats 原生绘制，Pulse 负责账号选择、额度数据与预警判断。
- 添加中英文界面和更新记录；保留 MIT 与 Apache-2.0 的组件许可证边界。

提供 Apple silicon 的 DMG、ZIP 和 SHA-256 校验文件。此构建使用临时签名，未经 Apple 公证；macOS 可能阻止从互联网下载的副本运行。请先查看仓库中的开源代码、许可证和安装说明。

## English

- Integrates Pulse AI quota monitoring into AirStats, including provider/account setup, quota windows, usage history, and alert settings.
- Adds compact Ring & Percentage and Multi-window Rings styles under Settings → Menu Bar → AI Usage → Display Style. The default ring shows the current quota; the multi-window style shows up to two quota windows.
- The status item is drawn by AirStats; Pulse remains responsible for account selection, quota data, and warning state.
- Includes bilingual UI and release notes while preserving the MIT and Apache-2.0 component license boundaries.

DMG, ZIP, and SHA-256 files are provided for Apple silicon. This build is ad-hoc signed and is not notarized by Apple; macOS may block an internet-downloaded copy. Review the source, licenses, and installation notes before use.
